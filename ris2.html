Creating Timeless Visual Poetry

For over fifteen years, I have dedicated myself to exploring the boundaries between the tangible and the ephemeral.

My practice combines traditional techniques with contemporary digital processes, creating works that exist at the intersection of memory and imagination.

Each piece is an invitation to pause, to contemplate, to discover something within yourself that resonates with the work.
